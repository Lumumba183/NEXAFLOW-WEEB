## MY HONEST RECOMMENDATION FOR YOUR BUSINESS

### Scenario A: You Build Static/React Sites + Want to Offer Email

**Best Setup:**
1. **Keep Netlify** for hosting your React/Vite static sites (it's free and perfect for this)
2. **Buy email hosting separately** from HostPinnacle or TrueHost
3. Point your client's domain MX records to the email host
4. Keep the website on Netlify

**Why:** You get the best static hosting (Netlify) + professional emails (Kenyan host). But this means managing TWO services per client.

### Scenario B: You Want ONE Place for Everything (Hosting + Email)

**Best Setup:**
1. **HostPinnacle Unlimited Plan** — KES 2,875/year

**Why:** One dashboard, unlimited everything, M-Pesa payments, and you build a recurring revenue stream.
