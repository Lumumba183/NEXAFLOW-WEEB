Web App vs Native App: What's the Difference?

You might be wondering: "Can't I just make my website work like an app?" Yes — that's called a Progressive Web App (PWA). But there's a big difference:

  • Web Apps (PWA): Cheaper, faster to build, work on all devices. But limited push notifications, no app store presence, and clunky offline access.
  • Native Android Apps: Faster, smoother, full push notifications, works offline, listed on Google Play Store, better M-Pesa integration. But costs more and takes longer.

Our recommendation: Start with a native Android app if your budget is KSH 50,000+. The experience is significantly better, and being on the Google Play Store adds massive credibility.
