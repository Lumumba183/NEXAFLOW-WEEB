## 📸 How to Generate These Images in Gemini

1. Go to <a href="https://gemini.google.com">gemini.google.com</a>
2. Click the "+" or image icon in the chat
3. Type or paste the prompt above
4. Add this at the end of your prompt: `, 16:9 aspect ratio, high resolution, professional blog hero image`
5. Gemini will generate the image
6. Download and save with the suggested filename
7. Upload to your website (replace the Unsplash placeholder URLs in the articles)

**Pro tip:** If the first result isn't perfect, ask Gemini to "make it more [specific]" or "change the colors to match [your brand colors]". You can also ask for variations.
